Stay Safe

Your safety matters. Here are some general steps you may consider.

📞 Contact Emergency Services

If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services. In India, you can call 112 for emergency assistance.

🤝 Talk to Someone You Trust

Consider sharing your situation with a trusted friend, family member, teacher or mentor.

📁 Keep Relevant Information

If it is safe to do so, keep relevant messages or documents that may help explain the situation.

⚖ Understand Your Rights

You deserve to be treated with dignity and respect. Consider seeking guidance from an appropriate support service.
